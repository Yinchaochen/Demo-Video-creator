# Storyboard and copy

Plan in a table before building: film time, bar, what the music does, picture, copy, sound. Times come from the music map, so the plan is
already synchronised when you start building.

## Copy

- One idea per line, short enough to read in the time. Allow about 0.4 s per word as a floor.
- Say what the product does for the person, using concrete verbs.
- Take the tagline, button text and fine print straight from the product's site. Those words are already approved.
- Name pains in general terms. Do not disparage competitors.
- Use the sentence case the brand uses. Mark one accent word per headline.
- Numbers need a source: the site, or something measured in this recording. State where a measured number came from in small type.

## Scene craft

- Start a scene on a bar line, finish its main motion before the next.
- Open wide, then push in to the part the headline names.
- Dim what does not matter instead of covering it, and ring what does.
- Carry something across a cut when you can. A logo that flies to the same logo in the app's header makes the jump from title to product feel continuous.
- Give the calm scene room. A trust message lands best when the score drops.
- Show the product's real speed honestly. Speed up waiting, and let a clock admit it.
